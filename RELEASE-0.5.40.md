# V.I.P LOGISTICS TRANSPORT DELIVERY APP v0.5.40

## Dashboard Fuel Gauge & Truck Indicators

This release carries forward the stable v0.5.39 delivery pause/resume system and adds dashboard telemetry improvements.

### Added
- Visual fuel gauge with fuel percentage, litres and maximum capacity.
- Fuel gauge colors based on fuel level:
  - Green: 75–100%
  - Red: 31–74%
  - Orange: 16–30%
  - Bright red: 0–15%
- Real TruckTel/ETS2 indicator status for:
  - Left indicator
  - Right indicator
  - Hazard lights
  - Parking lights
  - Low beam
  - High beam
  - Brake
  - Beacon

### Preserved
- Automatic delivery start/completion.
- Delivery pause/resume when switching ETS2 profiles.
- Cumulative delivery KM without contaminating one delivery with another profile's KM.
- TruckTel and ETS2 telemetry support.
- Existing auto-update/release workflow.
