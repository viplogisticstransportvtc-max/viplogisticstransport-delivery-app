# V.I.P LOGISTICS TRANSPORT DELIVERY APP v0.5.41

## Automatic update installation

- Checks for updates shortly after the packaged app starts.
- Downloads available updates automatically.
- Installs the downloaded update automatically and restarts the app.
- The current delivery is not completed or cancelled by the update restart.
- Delivery state remains stored on the backend and is restored after the app starts again.
- Existing telemetry, automatic delivery switching, pause/resume, fuel gauge, and indicators are preserved.
